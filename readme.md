**Slide Caliper**

I often build hardware projects, which require accurate measurements! That's why I thought that depending on a normal scale isn't feasible anymore! So I decided to make a measuring tool, a slide calipers of my own!!! This slide calipers 3d model is made entirely by me and We can print out Main scale and vernier scale paper strips and put them on the printed model to achieve our desired level of accuracy. So, this model can be used by anyone to get correct measurements in any metric according to their choice and needs
